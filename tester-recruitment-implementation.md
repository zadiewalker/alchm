## 🚨 Ethical Recruitment Guidelines

### Trauma-Informed Recruitment Principles

**1. Informed Consent:**
- Clear explanation of what testing involves
- Option to stop testing at any time
- No pressure tactics or urgency language
- Transparent about data usage

**2. Safety First:**
- Include crisis resources in all communications
- Respect confidentiality and anonymity
- Acknowledge testing may involve emotional content
- Provide easy opt-out mechanisms

**3. Community Respect:**
- Get admin approval before posting in support groups
- Follow community guidelines strictly
- Avoid exploiting vulnerable spaces for recruitment
- Offer value to communities, not just extraction

### Recruitment Ethics Checklist

Before any outreach:
- [ ] Message reviewed for pressure/manipulation
- [ ] Crisis resources included where appropriate
- [ ] Consent and opt-out clearly explained
- [ ] Community guidelines reviewed and followed
- [ ] Value proposition clear (helping others, not just us)
