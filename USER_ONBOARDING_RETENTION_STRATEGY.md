# ALCHM User Onboarding & Retention Strategy
*Trauma-Informed User Journey Design for Sustainable Growth*

## 🎯 Onboarding Philosophy

### Trauma-Informed Principles
- **Safety First**: No pressure, no time limits
- **Choice & Control**: Users direct their own experience  
- **Transparency**: Clear expectations and boundaries
- **Cultural Responsiveness**: Honor diverse backgrounds
- **Collaborative**: Partner with users, don't direct them

### Success Metrics
- **Quality over Quantity**: Deep engagement vs. high numbers
- **Emotional Safety**: Users feel safe to be vulnerable
- **Authentic Growth**: Real transformation, not addictive usage
- **Professional Integration**: Complements therapy, doesn't replace

## 🌱 First-Time User Journey

### Pre-Registration: Setting Expectations
**Landing Experience:**
```
Welcome to ALCHM
Your trauma-informed sanctuary for growth

This is not:
• A quick fix or miracle cure  
• A replacement for professional therapy
• A space where you'll be judged or diagnosed

This is:
• A safe space to process emotions
• An AI companion trained in trauma-informed care
• Your private sanctuary (your data stays yours)
• A complement to your existing healing journey

Ready to begin? Take as much time as you need.
```

### Registration: Gentle Information Gathering
**Step 1: Basic Information**
- Display name (can be anonymous)
- Email (for account recovery only)
- Timezone (for appropriate response timing)

**Step 2: Healing Context (Optional)**
```
Help us understand your journey (all optional):

• Are you currently working with a therapist? [Yes/No/Prefer not to say]
• What brings you to ALCHM today? [Open text, optional]
• How would you describe your current emotional state? [Scale + optional text]
• Are there specific triggers we should be aware of? [Optional, private]

Skip any questions that don't feel right for you today.
```

**Step 3: Privacy & Safety**
```
Your Privacy & Safety

✓ Your journal entries are encrypted and private
✓ We never sell or share your personal data
✓ You can delete your account and data anytime
✓ Crisis resources are available 24/7
✓ You control what you share and when

[Review Full Privacy Policy] [Continue]
```

**Gentle Reminders (Never Pushy):**
```

It's been a few days since your last visit. 
No pressure - just wanted you to know your space is here whenever you're ready.

Sometimes healing needs a pause. Trust your rhythm.

[Open ALCHM] [Remind Me Tomorrow] [Take a Break]
```

**For Users Who Haven't Returned (Day 3):**
```

Your sanctuary will be here whenever you're ready.

With care,
Zadie (ALCHM Creator)

```

## 📊 Retention Metrics (Trauma-Informed)

### Healthy Engagement Indicators
**Quality Metrics (Not Vanity Metrics):**
- Average session depth (meaningful writing time)
- Emotional range expression (healthy processing)
- Crisis resource effectiveness (when accessed)
- Pathway completion with reflection
- Professional therapy complement success

**Red Flag Metrics (Unhealthy Usage):**
- Compulsive daily usage (multiple hours)
- Crisis resource over-dependence
- Emotional escalation patterns
- Social isolation increase
- Therapy replacement behaviors

### Intervention Strategies
**For Concerning Usage Patterns:**
```
Gentle Check-In

We've noticed you've been spending a lot of time in ALCHM lately. 
Sometimes intensive journaling can be a sign of needing additional support.

Consider:
• Reaching out to a trusted friend
• Connecting with a professional therapist
• Taking a break from intensive processing
• Accessing crisis resources if needed

Your well-being matters more than app engagement.

[Find Local Support] [Contact Crisis Resources] [I'm Okay] [Take a Break]
```
