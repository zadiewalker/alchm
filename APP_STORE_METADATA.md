## Description
ALCHM is a quiet space for self-reflection. Journal your thoughts, track your moods, and meet Khepera — an AI companion who reflects back what you might not see on your own.

Unlike other wellness apps, ALCHM has no streaks, no scores, and no guilt. It is a sanctuary — a place to process what you carry, at your own pace, in your own words.

FEATURES
- Freeform journaling with mood tracking
- AI-powered reflections from Khepera
- Guided journaling pathways
- Evening check-in for lighter days
- Full data export
- Crisis resources (988 Lifeline) always accessible

IMPORTANT
ALCHM is a self-reflection tool, not a medical device. Khepera is an AI companion — not a therapist or medical professional. ALCHM does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.

In crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line).

PRIVACY
Journal entries are stored locally on your device. When you request a Khepera reflection, entry content is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) for processing.
