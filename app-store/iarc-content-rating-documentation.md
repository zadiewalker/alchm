# ALCHM - IARC Content Rating Documentation

## Application Information
- **App Name:** ALCHM - Identity Operating System
- **Developer:** ThirtyThree6 Technologies
- **Platform:** iOS & Android
- **Category:** Health & Fitness (Mental Health subcategory)

## IARC Rating Questionnaire Responses

### **Age Rating: 17+ (Mature)**

### **Content Categories Assessment**

#### **Violence**
- **Level:** None
- **Justification:** ALCHM contains no violent content, images, or gameplay elements. The application is focused entirely on journaling and self-reflection.

#### **Sexual Content**
- **Level:** None
- **Justification:** No sexual content is present in the application. While users may write about personal experiences including relationships, the app itself contains no sexual imagery or content.

#### **Language**
- **Level:** Mild
- **Justification:** The app may contain mild strong language in educational content about trauma recovery and mental health topics. User-generated content (journal entries) may contain strong language, but this is private to each user.

#### **Fear/Horror**
- **Level:** None
- **Justification:** No horror or fear-inducing content. While trauma-informed care deals with difficult topics, content is presented in a supportive, healing-focused manner.

#### **Gambling**
- **Level:** None
- **Justification:** No gambling content, simulated gambling, or in-game purchases that could be construed as gambling.

#### **Drugs/Alcohol**
- **Level:** Mild References
- **Justification:** Mental health and trauma recovery content may include educational references to substance use in recovery contexts. No promotion or detailed depictions of drug/alcohol use.

#### **Mature/Suggestive Themes**
- **Level:** Moderate
- **Justification:** ALCHM deals with mature psychological themes including:
  - Trauma recovery and healing
  - Identity exploration including LGBTQ+ topics
  - Mental health challenges and crisis situations
  - Complex emotional and psychological content
  - Suicide prevention and crisis intervention

### **Specific Content Considerations**

#### **Mental Health Content**
**Mature Themes Present:**
- Crisis detection and suicide prevention features
- Trauma-informed care approaches
- Discussion of mental health conditions
- Emotional regulation and psychological development
- Identity exploration including gender and sexuality

**Educational/Therapeutic Context:**
All content is presented within:
- Evidence-based trauma-informed frameworks
- Educational rather than sensationalized context
- Support and healing-focused perspective
- Professional crisis resource integration

#### **AI-Generated Content**
**Content Moderation:**
- AI responses are trained on therapeutic and educational content
- Built-in safety filters prevent harmful or inappropriate AI responses
- Trauma-informed response protocols prevent re-traumatization
- Human oversight of AI training and response patterns

#### **User-Generated Content**
**Private Nature:**
- All journal entries are private to individual users
- No public posting or sharing of journal content
- Community features use anonymous, privacy-preserving technology
- No user-to-user content sharing without explicit consent

#### **Crisis Support Features**
**Mature But Necessary:**
- Crisis detection algorithms for user safety
- Integration with emergency services when necessary
- Mental health resource recommendations
- Suicide prevention protocols and hotline access

### **Age Rating Justification: 17+**

#### **Why 17+ Rating is Appropriate:**

1. **Mature Psychological Themes**
   - Content deals with complex trauma, identity, and mental health topics
   - Requires emotional maturity to process therapeutic content appropriately
   - Crisis intervention features deal with life-threatening situations

2. **Identity Exploration Content**
   - Includes LGBTQ+ identity exploration features
   - Addresses complex questions of sexuality, gender, and personal identity
   - Requires maturity to engage with identity development safely

3. **Trauma-Informed Content**
   - Designed specifically for trauma survivors who have developed coping mechanisms
   - Requires understanding of trauma dynamics and healing processes
   - Content may trigger trauma responses in unprepared individuals

4. **Crisis Intervention Features**
   - Includes suicide prevention and crisis detection
   - Connects users with emergency services when necessary
   - Requires maturity to engage with crisis resources appropriately

5. **Therapeutic Depth**
   - Provides psychological insights that require mature interpretation
   - Deals with complex emotional and behavioral patterns
   - Requires capacity for self-reflection and personal responsibility

#### **Why Not 18+:**
- Content is educational and therapeutic rather than explicit
- AI features include safety guardrails and trauma-informed responses
- Professional oversight ensures age-appropriate therapeutic approaches
- Does not contain explicit sexual content, graphic violence, or substance abuse promotion

#### **Why Not Lower Rating:**
- Mental health content requires emotional maturity
- Trauma themes may be triggering for younger users
- Identity exploration features require developed sense of self
- Crisis intervention features deal with serious mental health emergencies
