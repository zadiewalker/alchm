**Safe, Private Journaling for Emotional Wellness**

ALCHM is a trauma-informed journaling platform designed to provide a safe digital space for reflection, emotional processing, and personal growth. Built with privacy-first principles and cultural responsiveness for diverse communities.

**🌿 CORE FEATURES**
• Free forever journaling with intelligent prompts
• Privacy-preserving AI insights (premium)
• Multi-language support (English, Spanish, Portuguese, Korean, Hindi, German)
• Offline journaling capability
• Crisis resource connections and safety features
• End-to-end encryption for maximum privacy

**🔒 PRIVACY & SAFETY FIRST**
• Your journal entries are never shared or sold
• Crisis detection with immediate resource connections
• Professional mental health resource referrals
• GDPR, CCPA, and privacy law compliant
• Local data processing when possible

**🌍 CULTURALLY RESPONSIVE DESIGN**
• Built with input from diverse communities
• Trauma-informed design principles throughout

**⚠️ IMPORTANT MEDICAL DISCLAIMER**
ALCHM is NOT therapy, medical treatment, or professional mental health care. This app provides journaling tools and wellness content only. For mental health diagnosis, treatment, or crisis intervention, please consult licensed healthcare professionals.

• 911 for emergencies
• Local crisis support services
