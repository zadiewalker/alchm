# ALCHM App Icon Specification

## Required Icon Sizes

### iOS App Store
- **1024x1024px** - App Store listing (PNG, no transparency)
- **180x180px** - iPhone app icon (@3x)
- **120x120px** - iPhone app icon (@2x)  
- **167x167px** - iPad Pro app icon
- **152x152px** - iPad app icon (@2x)
- **76x76px** - iPad app icon (@1x)

### Additional Sizes (System Use)
- **87x87px** - iPhone Settings (@3x)
- **58x58px** - iPhone Settings (@2x)
- **40x40px** - iPhone Spotlight (@2x)
- **60x60px** - iPhone Spotlight (@3x)
- **29x29px** - iPhone Settings (@1x)

## Design Concept

### Visual Identity
- **Primary Symbol**: Stylized ALCHM wordmark or abstract sanctuary symbol
- **Color Palette**: 
  - Primary: Sage green (#a4b792)
  - Accent: Warm gold (#d4af37)
  - Background: Soft white or light sage
- **Style**: Modern, welcoming, professional

### Design Principles
1. **Simplicity**: Clean, recognizable at small sizes
2. **Accessibility**: High contrast, clear visibility
3. **Cultural Sensitivity**: Inclusive, non-specific spiritual imagery
4. **Professional**: Conveys safety and trustworthiness
5. **Memorable**: Distinctive in App Store environment

### Icon Concepts

#### Option 1: ALCHM Wordmark Icon
- Clean typography treatment of "ALCHM"
- Sage green text on light background
- Subtle gradient or texture
- Professional, medical-appropriate styling

#### Option 2: Sanctuary Symbol
- Abstract symbol representing safe space
- Geometric or organic shapes suggesting protection
- Minimal design suitable for small sizes
- Cultural neutrality while suggesting healing

#### Option 3: Combined Approach
- ALCHM initial "A" as primary element
- Integrated with subtle sanctuary/healing symbol
- Balanced text and icon elements
- Professional color treatment

## Technical Requirements

### File Specifications
- **Format**: PNG (24-bit, no transparency for App Store)
- **Color Space**: sRGB
- **Quality**: Lossless compression
- **Corners**: iOS will apply rounded corners automatically
- **Effects**: No drop shadows, outer glows, or transparency

### Design Guidelines
- **No text smaller than 6pt** when rendered at final size
- **High contrast elements** for visibility
- **Consistent visual weight** across all sizes
- **Sharp edges and clean lines**
- **Professional medical app aesthetic**

### Platform-Specific Considerations

#### iOS Guidelines
- No transparency in App Store icon (1024x1024)
- System applies rounded corners automatically
- Icon should work well with rounded corners
- Consider how icon appears in various contexts (Settings, Spotlight, etc.)

#### App Store Presentation
- Icon appears in search results
- Shown alongside description and screenshots
- Must be professional and trustworthy
- Should differentiate from other mental health apps

## Brand Consistency

### Color Usage
- **Primary Sage**: Main brand color, calming and natural
- **Accent Gold**: Warmth and wisdom, sparingly used
- **Supporting Neutrals**: Whites, off-whites for balance
- **Avoid**: Bright reds (except for crisis elements), harsh colors

### Typography (if text included)
- **Font Style**: Clean, modern sans-serif
- **Weight**: Medium to bold for visibility
- **Kerning**: Optimized for small sizes
- **Hierarchy**: Clear primary element

### Symbol Guidelines
- **Cultural Neutrality**: Avoid specific religious or cultural symbols
- **Universal Appeal**: Recognizable across cultures
- **Positive Association**: Suggests growth, healing, safety
- **Scalability**: Works at all required sizes

## Accessibility Considerations

### Visual Accessibility
- **Contrast Ratio**: Minimum 3:1 for large elements
- **Color Independence**: Design readable without color
- **Clear Shapes**: Distinct, recognizable forms
- **Size Consideration**: Readable at 29x29px minimum

### Cognitive Accessibility
- **Simple Design**: Easy to recognize and remember
- **Familiar Elements**: Uses recognizable visual language
- **Consistent Branding**: Matches app's overall aesthetic
- **Clear Purpose**: Suggests app function appropriately

## Competitive Analysis

### Mental Health App Icons
- **Differentiation**: Stand out from therapy/meditation apps
- **Professional Positioning**: More professional than consumer wellness
- **Safety Communication**: Suggests crisis support capability
- **Cultural Inclusivity**: Broader appeal than traditional therapy apps

### Design Trends
- **Minimalism**: Clean, simple designs perform well
- **Gradients**: Subtle gradients add depth without complexity
- **Geometric Shapes**: Professional, trustworthy appearance
- **Natural Colors**: Earth tones suggest healing and growth

## Notes

### Crisis App Considerations
- Icon should suggest safety and support
- Avoid alarming or medical emergency aesthetics
- Balance professional help with accessibility
- Consider how icon appears during crisis situations

### Cultural Sensitivity
- Avoid symbols specific to any one tradition
- Ensure inclusive representation
- Test with diverse user groups
- Consider global market appropriateness

This icon specification ensures ALCHM's visual identity is professional, accessible, culturally sensitive, and compliant with App Store requirements while effectively representing the app's trauma-informed, crisis-safe mental health support mission.
