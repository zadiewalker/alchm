## Crisis-Specific Review Preparation

### Potential Review Questions & Responses

#### Crisis Safety Questions
**Q: "How does your app handle mental health emergencies?"**
**Prepared Response**: 
"ALCHM implements a comprehensive crisis intervention system:
- AI-powered crisis detection using anonymized emotional patterns
- Immediate one-tap access to 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
- Direct connection to emergency services (911)
- Professional medical disclaimers prominently displayed
- Clear boundaries about app limitations and need for professional care
- 24/7 crisis resource availability, including offline access"

#### Medical Treatment Boundaries
**Q: "What safeguards prevent users from viewing this as medical treatment?"**
**Prepared Response**:
"ALCHM includes comprehensive medical disclaimers:
- Prominent 'Not Medical Treatment' disclaimers throughout the app
- Required acknowledgment during onboarding
- Professional care referrals in all crisis contexts
- AI limitations clearly communicated
- Licensed therapist finder integration
- Crisis resources connect to professional services"

#### Age Verification
**Q: "How do you prevent access by users under 17?"**
**Prepared Response**:
"ALCHM enforces strict age verification:
- Required age verification before any account creation
- COPPA-compliant data handling procedures
- Immediate data deletion if underage user discovered
- Enhanced protections for 17-18 year olds
- Educational context safeguards for young adults"

### Review Response Templates

#### If Rejected for Medical Claims
**Response Strategy**:
1. Emphasize educational and journaling focus
2. Highlight comprehensive medical disclaimers
3. Document professional care referrals
4. Clarify AI limitations and boundaries
5. Provide crisis intervention documentation

#### If Rejected for Age Rating
**Response Strategy**:
1. Provide detailed content analysis
2. Justify 17+ rating with specific examples
3. Document crisis intervention appropriateness
4. Show COPPA compliance measures
5. Demonstrate educational value for young adults
