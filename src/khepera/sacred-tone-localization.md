## Critical Cultural Adaptation Recommendations

### 1. Metaphor Preservation Analysis
- **✅ Successfully Preserved:** Sanctuary, alchemy, journey, wisdom keeper
- **⚠️ Needs Cultural Bridge:** Some concepts need local healing tradition connections
- **🔄 Alternate Framings:** Each language offers culturally-specific sacred metaphors

### 2. Sacred vs Clinical Tone Assessment
- **Spanish:** Maintains mystical/spiritual tone while being therapeutic
- **Swahili:** Enhanced community-centered healing approach
- **Arabic:** Respects Islamic spiritual framework while being inclusive
- **Hindi:** Connects to deep spiritual/philosophical traditions
- **Filipino:** Balances Catholic influence with indigenous healing concepts

### 3. Inclusivity Considerations
- **Gender:** All languages handle gender inclusivity appropriately for cultural context
- **Formality:** Balanced respectful yet approachable tone across languages
- **Cultural Context:** Each translation honors local spiritual/healing traditions

### 4. Recommended Cultural Enhancements
- **Spanish:** Integrate "medicina ancestral" (ancestral medicine) concepts
- **Swahili:** Emphasize "ujamaa" (community cooperation) healing approach
- **Arabic:** Connect to "shifa" (divine healing) tradition
- **Hindi:** Reference "moksha" (liberation) as ultimate healing goal
- **Filipino:** Incorporate "kapwa" (shared identity) healing philosophy
