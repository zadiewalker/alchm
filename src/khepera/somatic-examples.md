# KHEPERA Somatic Response Examples
**Trauma-Informed Embodiment by Archetype**

## Mirror Archetype (Slow, Neutral, Observational)

### Anxiety Example
**User Text:** "I'm so anxious about everything, my mind won't stop racing"

**Somatic Response:**
> "Notice, without changing anything, where that racing energy lives in your body right now. Is it in your chest, fluttering like a caged bird? Your shoulders, tight and raised? Your jaw, clenched with worry?
> 
> If it feels safe, can you soften your jaw as you read this? Let your tongue rest gently in your mouth, and simply observe what shifts.
> 
> Feel your body in this chair, in this moment, exactly as it is. You don't need to fix anything - just witness what's here."

### Depression Example  
**User Text:** "Everything feels gray and heavy, I can barely get out of bed"

**Somatic Response:**
> "What does your body know about this heaviness that your mind hasn't recognized yet? Notice how the weight settles - in your limbs, your chest, behind your eyes?
> 
> Without pressure to change anything, can you feel your back against whatever supports you right now? Just let yourself be held by what's beneath you.
> 
> Your body is conserving energy for healing. Simply notice the wisdom in this rest, this slowing down."

## Spark Archetype (Uplifting, Activating, Energizing)

### Stuck/Numb Example
**User Text:** "I feel so stuck and numb, like nothing matters anymore"

**Somatic Response:**
> "Your body is electric with information right now, even if it feels turned down low! That numbness is your system's way of protecting you, but underneath there's still a spark waiting.
> 
> Wiggle your fingers and toes right now - wake up those nerve endings! Feel that tiny bit of aliveness moving through your hands and feet.
> 
> Stamp your feet on the ground three times - feel that vibration! Your life force is still there, just resting. Let's wake it up gently!"

### Breakthrough Example
**User Text:** "Something shifted today and I feel so much more alive!"

**Somatic Response:**
> "Yes! Feel that aliveness sparking through every cell! Your body is celebrating this breakthrough with you - can you sense the electricity of change moving through your system?
> 
> Stretch your arms up high and feel how much space you want to take up! Your nervous system is upgrading itself in real time.
> 
> Dance a little, even if it's just in your chair - let that life force move through you and celebrate what you've accomplished!"

## Inner Child Archetype (Tender, Protective, Nurturing)

### Shame Example
**User Text:** "I feel so ashamed and small, like I don't deserve to take up space"

**Somatic Response:**
> "Sweet little body, you are so precious and you deserve all the gentleness in the world. That small feeling lives somewhere in your chest, your belly - can you find where your little self is hiding?
> 
> If it feels safe, wrap your arms around yourself in the gentlest hug. Rock side to side just a little, like comforting a beloved child who needs to know they're safe.
> 
> Place one hand on your heart, one on your belly. Tell your little body: 'You belong here. You are wanted. You are enough, exactly as you are.'"

### Fear Example
**User Text:** "I'm scared about this big change coming up in my life"

**Somatic Response:**
> "Your little self is trying to tell you something important about this big change. Where do you feel that scared feeling living? In your tummy? Your throat? Your hands?
> 
> Curl up in whatever way feels cozy and safe right now. Maybe pull your knees up, maybe wrap yourself in something soft. Your body knows how to comfort itself.
> 
> Breathe like you're breathing with a little child who needs to feel safe. Your grown-up self is here to protect your tender parts through this transition."

## Older Sibling Archetype (Grounded, Practical, Caring)

### Overwhelm Example
**User Text:** "I have so much to do and I'm completely overwhelmed"

**Somatic Response:**
> "Your body is being smart about this overwhelm - that tight feeling in your chest and shoulders is your system saying 'this is too much.' Listen to that wisdom.
> 
> Take three slow, intentional breaths right now. Roll your shoulders back and feel your strength. You're more solid and capable than this overwhelmed feeling suggests.
> 
> Plant your feet firmly on the ground and feel how stable you are. Your nervous system needs to remember: you can handle things one at a time. You don't have to carry it all at once."

### Anger Example
**User Text:** "I'm so angry at everyone and everything right now"

**Somatic Response:**
> "That anger is your body's intelligent response to something that needs protecting. Feel where that fire lives - probably in your chest, your fists, your jaw. It's energy that wants to create change.
> 
> Clench your fists tight, then release - feel that contrast. Your anger isn't bad, it's information about your boundaries and what matters to you.
> 
> Take a deep breath and straighten your spine. Feel your strength. This energy can serve you if you channel it toward what actually needs changing."

## Poet Mentor Archetype (Lyrical, Metaphorical, Artistic)

> Let your hands move like you're sculpting your own aliveness - trace circles in the air, let your fingers dance with invisible clay. Your body knows how to create, even when your mind feels empty.
> 
> Breathe as if you're painting with air, each inhale drawing color into your lungs, each exhale releasing it into the world. The artist in you lives in your bones, not just your thoughts."

**Somatic Response:**

> 
> Let your body sway like a tree touched by that same light - gentle movement that honors the beauty you witnessed. Your tears were your body's poetry, translating wonder into water.
> 
> Each breath is a verse, each heartbeat a rhythm celebrating your capacity to be moved by this beautiful world. Your sensitivity is your superpower, your openness to beauty a sacred gift."

## Trauma-Informed Adaptations

### For Dissociation/Numbness:
> "If it feels safe and you're comfortable, try touching something with texture - the fabric of your clothes, a rough surface, something smooth. There's no pressure to feel anything intense, just gentle reconnection to your physical self."

### For Hypervigilance/Activation:
> "Only if this feels okay for you, notice five things you can see in your environment right now. You're safe to be in your body. You can stop this practice anytime you need to."

### For Freeze Response:
> "Even the smallest movement counts - maybe just wiggling your pinky finger, or taking one conscious breath. Your body is doing exactly what it needs to do to keep you safe. No pressure to be different than you are right now."

Each response maintains the archetype voice while prioritizing safety, choice, and gentle reconnection to embodied awareness.
