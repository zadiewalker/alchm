# ALCHM Sacred Microcopy Examples

> *Interface language as emotional architecture - every word holds, nurtures, and affirms rather than directs*

### **Terracotta** 🟤 - Grounding & Affirmation
*Earthy, rooted, affirming language that connects users to their inner strength*

### **Charcoal** ⚫ - Boundary & Depth
*Protective, serious, profound language that establishes clear sacred boundaries*

*Gentle, open, compassionate language that honors vulnerability as strength*

## 💝 Implementation Guidelines

### **Tone Selection Framework:**

1. **Terracotta (Grounding/Affirmation)** - Use for:
   - Primary actions that empower
   - Confirmations of growth/progress  
   - Navigation and orientation
   - Milestone celebrations

2. **Charcoal (Boundary/Depth)** - Use for:
   - Serious actions (delete, logout)
   - Privacy and security matters
   - Profound reflections
   - Clear instructions with gravity

3. **Soft Blush (Vulnerability/Tenderness)** - Use for:
   - Error messages and guidance
   - Sharing and connection features
   - Gentle prompts and invitations
   - Emotional support moments

### **Context Adaptations:**

- **New Users:** Add more guidance and reassurance
- **Struggling Moments:** Increase tenderness and support language  
- **Celebration Times:** Amplify joy and recognition
- **Cultural Context:** Adapt metaphors and references appropriately
- **Time of Day:** Morning = opening, Evening = reflection, Night = rest

### **Writing Principles:**

1. **Agency First:** Language that empowers rather than directs
2. **Process Honoring:** Acknowledge the journey, not just outcomes
3. **Emotional Safety:** Create containers, not pressure
4. **Cultural Humility:** Honor diverse ways of knowing and being
5. **Trauma-Informed:** Assume healing is happening and support it

This sacred microcopy system transforms every interface interaction into a moment of emotional care, replacing the efficiency-focused language of traditional UX with the holding, affirming presence of a digital sanctuary.
