# FERPA Educational Records Notice
**ALCHM Digital Journaling Platform**

## Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) Compliance Notice

### What is FERPA?

The Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) is a federal privacy law that gives parents certain rights with respect to their children's educational records. These rights transfer to the student when they reach 18 years of age or attend a postsecondary institution.

### Educational Records in ALCHM

When ALCHM is used in educational settings, the following information is classified as educational records under FERPA:

#### Protected Educational Records:
- **Journal Entries**: All reflective writing and emotional check-ins
- **AI Interaction Logs**: Records of Khepera AI guidance sessions
- **Wellness Analytics**: Mood tracking and emotional progress data
- **Crisis Indicators**: Safety-related assessments and interventions
- **Learning Progress**: Educational insights derived from platform usage

#### Information NOT Considered Educational Records:
- **Technical Logs**: Server access logs and security events (no student content)
- **Aggregate Data**: Anonymized usage statistics across all users
- **Directory Information**: Basic school association (if designated by school)

### Your FERPA Rights

#### Right to Inspect and Review
- **Request Access**: You have the right to inspect and review your educational records within 45 days
- **How to Request**: Contact your school's FERPA officer or ALCHM's privacy team
- **What You'll Receive**: Complete copy of all educational records in digital format
- **Cost**: No charge for the first copy; additional copies may incur reasonable fees

#### Right to Request Amendment
- **Correction Process**: You may request correction of educational records you believe are inaccurate
- **Formal Request**: Submit written request to school FERPA officer with specific corrections
- **School Response**: School must respond within 30 days of receiving your request
- **Appeal Process**: If request is denied, you have the right to a formal hearing

#### Right to Control Disclosure
- **Prior Consent**: Generally required before educational records can be disclosed
- **Exceptions**: Limited circumstances where disclosure is permitted without consent
- **Disclosure Log**: Schools must maintain records of all disclosures made

### FERPA Exceptions - When We May Disclose Without Consent

ALCHM may disclose educational records without consent only in these specific circumstances:

#### School Officials with Legitimate Educational Interest
- **Authorized Personnel**: Teachers, counselors, administrators with educational need
- **Third-Party Contractors**: Service providers performing institutional services
- **Volunteer Requirements**: Background-checked volunteers in official capacity

#### Emergency Situations
- **Health and Safety**: Immediate danger to student or others
- **Crisis Intervention**: Mental health emergencies requiring intervention
- **Law Enforcement**: Specific criminal activities on school property

#### Legal Requirements
- **Court Orders**: Judicial orders requiring disclosure
- **Subpoenas**: Legal subpoenas (with advance notice when possible)
- **Federal Audits**: Department of Education compliance reviews

#### Transfer Situations
- **School Changes**: When student transfers to another educational institution
- **Records Requests**: From new school with legitimate educational need
- **Graduation**: Alumni records for verification purposes

### How ALCHM Protects Educational Records

#### Technical Safeguards
- **Encryption**: All educational records encrypted using AES-256 encryption
- **Access Controls**: Multi-factor authentication for all authorized users
- **Audit Trails**: Complete logging of all access to educational records
- **Data Segregation**: Student records isolated by institution and grade level

#### Administrative Safeguards
- **Training**: All staff trained on FERPA requirements and privacy protection
- **Background Checks**: Comprehensive screening of personnel with data access
- **Signed Agreements**: All employees and contractors sign FERPA compliance agreements
- **Regular Audits**: Annual third-party audits of FERPA compliance procedures

#### Physical Safeguards
- **Secure Facilities**: Data centers with 24/7 security and access controls
- **Redundant Backups**: Encrypted backups in geographically separate locations
- **Disaster Recovery**: Comprehensive business continuity planning
- **Secure Disposal**: Certified destruction of retired storage devices

### Directory Information

Schools may designate certain information as "directory information" under FERPA:

#### Potential Directory Information:
- Student name (if used in platform)
- School association
- Grade level or year of study
- Dates of attendance

#### Your Rights Regarding Directory Information:
- **Opt-Out**: Right to request that directory information not be disclosed
- **Annual Notice**: Schools must provide annual notice of directory information
- **Reasonable Time**: Schools must provide reasonable time to opt out

### Disclosure to Parents

#### Students Under 18:
- **Parental Rights**: Parents retain full FERPA rights for minor children
- **Record Access**: Parents can access all educational records
- **Consent Authority**: Parents provide consent for disclosures

#### Students 18 and Over:
- **Rights Transfer**: FERPA rights transfer to student at age 18
- **Parental Access**: Parents lose automatic access to records
- **Consent Required**: Student consent needed for parental access
- **Emergency Exception**: Health/safety emergencies may allow parental notification

### Data Retention Under FERPA

#### While Student is Enrolled:
- **Active Records**: Maintained for duration of enrollment
- **Regular Backups**: Encrypted backups for data protection
- **Version Control**: Historical versions maintained for accuracy

#### After Student Leaves:
- **Retention Period**: Determined by individual school district policy
- **Permanent Records**: Some records maintained permanently by school
- **ALCHM Deletion**: Platform data deleted according to school's retention schedule

### Complaints Process

#### How to File a FERPA Complaint:
1. **School Level**: First attempt to resolve with school's FERPA officer
2. **District Level**: Escalate to school district's privacy coordinator
3. **Federal Level**: File complaint with U.S. Department of Education
