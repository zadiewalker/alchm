# ALCHM Privacy Policy
**Effective Date:** January 2025  
**Last Updated:** January 2025

## Overview

ALCHM is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of all users, particularly students and minors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle your personal information in compliance with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and other applicable privacy laws.

## Who We Are

ALCHM is a trauma-informed, AI-powered journaling platform designed to support emotional wellness and spiritual growth. We operate as an educational technology service provider and are committed to maintaining the highest standards of privacy protection for students and educational institutions.

## Information We Collect

### Educational Records (FERPA Protected)
When used in educational settings, journal entries and related data are classified as educational records under FERPA. These include:

- **Journal Entries**: Reflective writing content (encrypted before storage)
- **Emotional Check-ins**: Mood and emotion data submitted voluntarily
- **Usage Patterns**: Learning analytics to improve educational outcomes
- **Crisis Indicators**: Safety-related information for student protection

### Personal Information (COPPA Protected)
For users under 13, we collect only the minimum information necessary:

- **Account Identifier**: Unique, non-identifying user ID
- **School Association**: Educational institution (if applicable)
- **Language Preference**: For culturally appropriate support
- **Parental Consent**: Documentation of parental permission

### Technical Information
- **Device Information**: Browser type, operating system (for security)
- **Usage Analytics**: Anonymized platform interaction data
- **Security Logs**: Access attempts and security events (no personal content)

## How We Protect Your Information

### Encryption and Security
- **Client-Side Encryption**: All journal content is encrypted on your device before transmission
- **AES-256 Encryption**: Military-grade encryption for all sensitive data
- **Zero-Knowledge Architecture**: We cannot read your encrypted journal content
- **Secure Transmission**: All data transmitted using TLS 1.3 encryption

### Data Minimization
- **Need-to-Know Basis**: Only authorized personnel access necessary data
- **Automatic Redaction**: Personal identifiers automatically removed from content
- **Session Isolation**: AI processing occurs in isolated sessions with no memory
- **Regular Deletion**: Unnecessary data automatically purged

### Access Controls
- **Multi-Factor Authentication**: Required for administrative access
- **Role-Based Permissions**: Staff access limited by job function
- **Audit Logging**: All data access events recorded and monitored
- **Regular Security Audits**: Third-party security assessments conducted annually

## How We Use Your Information

### Educational Purposes
- **Personalized Support**: Culturally appropriate AI guidance
- **Crisis Intervention**: Safety monitoring and resource provision
- **Learning Analytics**: Aggregate insights for educational improvement
- **Research**: Anonymized data for trauma-informed pedagogy research (with consent)

### AI Processing (Khepera)
- **Isolated Sessions**: Each interaction processed independently
- **No Memory**: AI has no access to previous conversations
- **Cultural Adaptation**: Responses tailored to user's cultural background
- **Safety Monitoring**: Automatic detection of crisis indicators

## Your Rights Under Privacy Laws

### FERPA Rights (Educational Records)
- **Inspection**: Right to review your educational records
- **Amendment**: Right to request correction of inaccurate records
- **Disclosure Control**: Right to control disclosure to third parties
- **Complaint**: Right to file complaints with the Department of Education

### COPPA Rights (Children Under 13)
- **Parental Consent**: Required before collecting any personal information
- **Parental Access**: Parents can review and request deletion of child's data
- **Limited Collection**: Only information necessary for service operation
- **No Behavioral Advertising**: No targeted advertising based on child's data

### GDPR Rights (EU Users)
- **Access**: Right to obtain copy of your personal data
- **Rectification**: Right to correct inaccurate personal data
- **Erasure**: Right to deletion of personal data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Portability**: Right to receive your data in machine-readable format
- **Objection**: Right to object to processing of personal data
- **Restriction**: Right to restrict processing under certain circumstances

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We Do NOT Share Your Information For:
- ❌ **Commercial Purposes**: No selling or renting of personal data
- ❌ **Marketing**: No targeted advertising or promotional activities
- ❌ **Data Mining**: No analysis for commercial insights
- ❌ **Third-Party Analytics**: No sharing with analytics companies

### We May Share Information Only For:
- ✅ **Safety Requirements**: Mandated reporting of imminent danger
- ✅ **Legal Compliance**: Court orders or legal process
- ✅ **Educational Services**: With school officials who have legitimate need
- ✅ **Parental Rights**: With parents/guardians for minor children

### Crisis Intervention Protocol
In cases of immediate danger to self or others, we may:
1. Display crisis resources appropriate to user's location and language
2. Connect with local emergency services if requested by user
3. Notify school counselors or designated officials (educational settings only)
4. Contact parents/guardians for minors (COPPA requirement)

## Data Retention and Deletion

### Automatic Deletion
- **Session Data**: Deleted immediately after session ends
- **Temporary Files**: Purged within 24 hours
- **Error Logs**: Anonymized after 30 days
- **Audit Logs**: Retained for 7 years for compliance (no personal content)

### User-Controlled Retention
- **Journal Entries**: User can set automatic deletion (30 days to 7 years)
- **Account Data**: Deleted within 30 days of account closure request
- **Backup Systems**: All copies removed within 90 days of deletion request

### Educational Records Retention
- **Active Students**: Records retained during enrollment
- **Graduated Students**: Deleted according to school district policy
- **Research Data**: Anonymized data may be retained for research with consent

## International Data Transfers

### Data Localization
- **Primary Storage**: Data stored in user's region when possible
- **EU Data**: Stored within EU for GDPR compliance
- **Cross-Border Transfers**: Only with adequate protection measures
- **Standard Contractual Clauses**: Used for international transfers
