# COPPA Children's Privacy Notice
**ALCHM Digital Journaling Platform**

## Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Compliance

### Notice to Parents and Legal Guardians

ALCHM is committed to protecting the privacy of children under 13 years of age. This notice explains our practices regarding children's personal information in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

### Important: Parental Consent Required

**We do not collect personal information from children under 13 without verified parental consent.**

If your child is under 13 and wishes to use ALCHM, you must provide consent through our verified parental consent process.

### What Information We Collect from Children Under 13

#### With Parental Consent, We May Collect:

**Account Information:**
- Unique user identifier (no real name required)
- School or educational institution association
- Grade level (for age-appropriate content)
- Language preference for cultural support

**Journal Content:**
- Reflective writing entries (encrypted before storage)
- Emotional check-ins and mood tracking
- Responses to AI guidance from Khepera
- Progress in emotional wellness activities

**Usage Information:**
- Platform interaction patterns (for educational improvement)
- Time spent in different activities
- Features used and learning progress
- Technical information needed for platform operation

#### Information We Do NOT Collect:
- ❌ Real names or personal identifiers
- ❌ Home addresses or phone numbers
- ❌ Email addresses or social media profiles
- ❌ Photos, videos, or audio recordings
- ❌ Location information beyond general region
- ❌ Information about friends or family members

### How We Use Children's Information

#### Educational Purposes Only:
- **Personalized Learning**: Culturally appropriate AI guidance
- **Emotional Support**: Age-appropriate wellness activities
- **Safety Monitoring**: Detection of potential crisis situations
- **Progress Tracking**: Educational insights for teachers and parents

#### What We Do NOT Do:
- ❌ **No Advertising**: No behavioral advertising to children
- ❌ **No Data Sales**: Never sell or rent children's information
- ❌ **No Social Features**: No public profiles or social networking
- ❌ **No Tracking**: No tracking across other websites or apps

### How We Protect Children's Information

#### Enhanced Security Measures:
- **Client-Side Encryption**: All journal content encrypted on child's device
- **Zero-Knowledge Design**: We cannot read encrypted journal entries
- **Access Controls**: Strict limits on who can access children's data
- **Automatic Deletion**: Unnecessary data automatically removed

#### Special Protections for Children:
- **No Adult Contact**: No direct communication between children and adults
- **Moderated AI**: Enhanced content filtering for age-appropriate responses
- **Crisis Protocols**: Immediate parent/school notification for safety concerns
- **Limited Data Sharing**: More restrictive sharing policies than for adults

### Parental Consent Process

#### How to Provide Consent:

**Step 1: Verification**
- Complete parental consent form with legal name and contact information
- Provide photo ID or other verification document
- Confirm parent-child relationship

**Step 2: Consent Methods** (Choose One)
- **Signed Form**: Print, sign, and upload completed consent form
- **Video Consent**: Record video consent using provided script
- **Credit Card Verification**: Small charge verification (immediately refunded)
- **Digital Signature**: Use certified digital signature service

**Step 3: Account Setup**
- Receive secure account creation link
- Set up child's account with approved school or teacher
- Configure privacy settings and notifications

#### Consent Documentation:
- **Record Keeping**: All consent records securely stored for account duration
- **Verification**: Consent verification email sent to provided address
- **Updates**: Annual consent confirmation required

### Parental Rights and Controls

#### Your Rights as a Parent:

**Access Rights:**
- **Review Information**: See all information collected about your child
- **Data Export**: Receive copy of your child's data in standard format
- **Access Logs**: View all access to your child's account

**Control Rights:**
- **Modify Settings**: Change privacy settings and data sharing preferences
- **Withdraw Consent**: Revoke consent and delete child's account
- **Communication Control**: Choose how and when we contact you
- **Feature Control**: Enable or disable specific platform features

**Notification Rights:**
- **Data Changes**: Advance notice of any changes to data practices
- **Security Incidents**: Immediate notification of any security issues
- **Educational Updates**: Regular reports on child's platform usage

#### How to Exercise Your Rights:

**Required Information:**

### Information Sharing and Disclosure

#### With Parental Consent, We May Share Information With:

**Educational Partners:**
- Teachers and school counselors with legitimate educational need
- School administrators for student safety and progress monitoring
- Educational technology staff for technical support

**Safety Partners:**
- Licensed mental health professionals in crisis situations
- Emergency services if child expresses intent to harm self or others
- Law enforcement only when required by law or court order

#### We Will Never Share Information For:
- ❌ **Marketing Purposes**: No promotional or advertising activities
- ❌ **Commercial Use**: No selling or licensing of children's data
- ❌ **Third-Party Analytics**: No sharing with data analytics companies
- ❌ **Social Media**: No integration with social media platforms

### Special Crisis Intervention Procedures for Children

#### Enhanced Safety Protocols:

**Crisis Detection:**
- **Automated Monitoring**: AI detection of concerning language or emotions
- **Human Review**: Trained professionals review all potential crisis indicators
- **Multiple Safeguards**: Redundant systems to ensure no crisis goes unnoticed

**Parent Notification:**
- **Immediate Contact**: Parents contacted within 1 hour of crisis detection
- **Multiple Methods**: Phone calls, text messages, and email notifications
- **Clear Information**: Detailed explanation of concern and recommended actions

**Professional Support:**
- **Licensed Counselors**: Access to qualified mental health professionals
- **School Coordination**: Collaboration with school counselors and administrators
- **Resource Provision**: Age-appropriate crisis resources and support materials

### Data Retention for Children Under 13

#### Automatic Deletion:
- **Session Data**: Deleted immediately after each session
- **Temporary Files**: Purged within 24 hours
- **Unused Accounts**: Deleted after 30 days of inactivity

#### Parental Control:
- **Custom Retention**: Parents can set data retention periods
- **Immediate Deletion**: Parents can request immediate deletion at any time
- **Graduation**: Data handling when child turns 13 or graduates

#### Legal Requirements:
- **Educational Records**: Retained according to school district policies
- **Safety Records**: Crisis intervention records retained as required by law
- **Consent Records**: Parental consent documentation retained per FTC requirements

### Age Verification and Monitoring

#### How We Verify Age:
- **School Registration**: Age verification through educational institution
- **Parent Declaration**: Parental confirmation of child's age
- **Regular Monitoring**: Periodic verification to ensure continued compliance

#### When Child Turns 13:
- **Rights Transfer**: Child gains control over their own account
- **Parent Notification**: 30-day advance notice before rights transfer
- **Consent Update**: Option to continue account with child's direct consent
- **Data Retention**: Opportunity to modify data retention preferences
