# ALCHM COMPREHENSIVE REGISTRATION AUDIT & COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

As the ALCHM Privacy & Legal Compliance Specialist, I have conducted a comprehensive audit of all registration, compliance, and certification requirements for operating a trauma-informed mental health platform serving vulnerable youth populations. This document provides a detailed roadmap for ensuring full regulatory compliance across all jurisdictions and platforms.

**CRITICAL FINDING**: ALCHM operates in a highly regulated environment requiring multiple layers of compliance, from business registration to specialized mental health platform requirements. Immediate attention is needed for youth protection measures and crisis intervention protocols.

## 1. BUSINESS & LEGAL REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS

### 1.1 Business Entity Formation
**IMMEDIATE ACTION REQUIRED**

- [ ] **Business Entity Registration**
  - Choose business structure (LLC recommended for liability protection)
  - Register in primary state of operation
  - Obtain Federal EIN from IRS
  - Register for state tax accounts
  - **Timeline**: 2-4 weeks
  - **Cost**: $100-$800 depending on state

- [ ] **Healthcare Business Licensing**
  - Research state-specific mental health service licensing requirements
  - Determine if ALCHM qualifies as "healthcare adjacent" requiring special licensing
  - Obtain required behavioral health organization licenses
  - **Timeline**: 3-6 months
  - **Cost**: $500-$2,500 per state

- [ ] **Professional Liability Insurance**
  - Secure malpractice/professional liability coverage
  - Minimum $1M coverage recommended
  - **Timeline**: 1-2 weeks
  - **Cost**: $2,000-$10,000 annually

### 1.2 Educational Requirements (2025 Updates)
**CRITICAL COMPLIANCE CHANGE**

Beginning July 1, 2025, new educational requirements take effect:
- Master's degree from CACREP-accredited program (60+ semester hours)
- Alternative: MPCAC accreditation or equivalent
- **Action**: Verify any staff providing mental health guidance meets requirements

## 2. PLATFORM REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS

### 2.1 Google Play Console Registration
**REQUIRED FOR ANDROID DEPLOYMENT**

- [ ] **Health Apps Declaration**
  - Complete mandatory Health Apps Declaration form
  - Declare mental health and journaling functionalities
  - **Deadline**: Must be completed before app publication
  - **Timeline**: 1-2 weeks

- [ ] **Organization Registration**
  - Register as Organization (not Individual) for health services
  - Provide business documentation
  - **Requirement**: Mandatory for health apps as of 2024

- [ ] **Privacy Disclosures**
  - Implement prominent health data use disclosures
  - Obtain affirmative user consent
  - Document data handling practices
  - **Compliance**: Required for Health Connect permissions

### 2.2 Apple App Store Registration
**REQUIRED FOR iOS DEPLOYMENT**

- [ ] **Apple Developer Program**
  - Enroll as Organization ($99/year)
  - Provide business verification documents
  - **Timeline**: 1-2 weeks

- [ ] **Enhanced Privacy Requirements (2025)**
  - Implement detailed permission explanations
  - Provide limited functionality without sensitive permissions
  - Document data usage and storage practices
  - **Critical**: Extra scrutiny for mental health apps

- [ ] **Medical App Review Preparation**
  - Prepare for stricter review process
  - Document any FDA or regulatory clearances
  - Avoid medical diagnosis/treatment claims
  - **Timeline**: Extended review period expected

## 3. COMPLIANCE & CERTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

### 3.1 COPPA Safe Harbor Program Enrollment
**CRITICAL FOR YOUTH PROTECTION**

- [ ] **Age Verification Systems**
  - Implement robust age-gating mechanisms
  - Design parental consent workflows
  - Create age-appropriate privacy notices
  - **Deadline**: June 23, 2025 (new rule effective date)

- [ ] **Safe Harbor Program Selection**
  - Evaluate COPPA Safe Harbor providers:
    - iKeepSafe COPPA Safe Harbor
    - PRIVO COPPA Safe Harbor
    - Kidoz COPPA Safe Harbor
  - **Timeline**: 2-3 months enrollment process
  - **Cost**: $1,500-$15,000 annually

- [ ] **Enhanced Transparency Requirements (2025)**
  - Prepare for public membership list disclosure
  - Document privacy and security policies
  - **Deadline**: July 21, 2025 for transparency requirements

### 3.2 HIPAA Compliance Framework
**NO OFFICIAL CERTIFICATION AVAILABLE**

- [ ] **Third-Party Certification Pursuit**
  - Consider HITRUST CSF certification
  - Evaluate ISO 27001 certification
  - **Timeline**: 6-12 months
  - **Cost**: $25,000-$100,000

- [ ] **Business Associate Agreements**
  - Execute BAAs with all third-party vendors
  - Cover: Firebase, Stripe, analytics providers, AI services
  - **Critical**: Must cover all entities handling PHI
  - **Timeline**: 4-6 weeks for all agreements

- [ ] **Administrative Safeguards**
  - Designate HIPAA Security Officer
  - Implement workforce training program
  - Establish incident response procedures
  - **Timeline**: 6-8 weeks implementation

### 3.3 GDPR Compliance (International Users)
**MANDATORY FOR EU USERS**

- [ ] **Data Processing Documentation**
  - Complete Article 30 processing records
  - Document lawful basis for each processing activity
  - Implement data subject rights workflows
  - **Timeline**: 8-12 weeks

- [ ] **Privacy Impact Assessments**
  - Conduct DPIA for high-risk processing
  - Document safeguards for vulnerable populations
  - **Required**: For youth mental health data processing
