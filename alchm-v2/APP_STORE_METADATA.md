## Description
ALCHM is a calm space for self-reflection. Write freely, track your mood, and receive gentle reflections from Khepera, an AI companion.

FEATURES
- Journal with mood and tags
- Guided pathways and evening check-ins
- Reflections from Khepera (AI-generated)
- Data export
- Crisis support is always accessible (call or text 988)

IMPORTANT
ALCHM is not therapy and does not provide medical advice. Khepera is an AI companion, not a therapist, counselor, or medical professional. ALCHM does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline). You can also text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line).

PRIVACY
Your journal entries are stored on your device. If you choose to enable cloud reflections by providing your own Anthropic API key, entry text is sent to Anthropic to generate a reflection.
