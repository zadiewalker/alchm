ALCHM Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: September 3, 2024

🚨 CRITICAL NOTICE: ALCHM is NOT a medical device, healthcare provider, or substitute for professional mental health treatment. This app is designed for wellness support and personal reflection only.

1. Not Medical Advice or Treatment

⚕️ Important: The content, features, and AI-generated insights provided by ALCHM are for informational and wellness support purposes only. They do not constitute:
  • Medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment
  • Professional mental health counseling or therapy
  • Crisis intervention or emergency mental health services
  • Psychiatric evaluation or assessment
  • Medication recommendations or management

2. AI System Limitations

Our AI-powered features:

  • Are not trained medical professionals and cannot provide medical diagnoses
  • Use pattern recognition based on your journal entries, not clinical assessment
  • May produce inaccurate insights and should not be relied upon for health decisions
  • Cannot replace human judgment in crisis situations
  • Are designed for wellness support, not therapeutic intervention

3. Crisis and Emergency Situations

🆘 If you are experiencing a mental health crisis:
  • Call 911 or your local emergency services immediately
  • Contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988
  • Go to your nearest emergency room
  • Call your mental health provider if you have one

Do not rely on ALCHM's crisis detection features as your primary safety plan.

4. Professional Care Recommendation

ALCHM strongly recommends that users:

  • Consult with qualified mental health professionals for any mental health concerns
  • Use ALCHM as a supplementary tool alongside professional care, not as a replacement
  • Discuss their use of ALCHM with their healthcare providers
  • Seek immediate professional help if experiencing distressing symptoms

5. Health Conditions and Contraindications

ALCHM may not be suitable for individuals with:

  • Severe mental illness requiring intensive treatment
  • Active suicidal ideation or self-harm behaviors
  • Substance use disorders in acute phases
  • Cognitive impairments that affect judgment
  • Conditions requiring immediate medical supervision

If any of these conditions apply to you, please consult with a healthcare professional before using ALCHM.

6. Data and Privacy in Healthcare Context

While ALCHM follows privacy best practices:

  • We are not a covered entity under HIPAA
  • Your data does not receive HIPAA-level protection
  • Consider the privacy implications before sharing sensitive health information
  • Consult with your healthcare provider about digital wellness tool usage

7. Age and Capacity Requirements

ALCHM is intended for:

  • Users 17 years of age and older
  • Individuals with the capacity to make informed decisions about their wellness
  • People who can distinguish between wellness support and medical treatment

  • Any health-related decisions made based on app usage

  • Inaccurate AI-generated insights or recommendations

  • Reliance on the app instead of seeking professional care
