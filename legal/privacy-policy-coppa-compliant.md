# ALCHM Privacy Policy
## Last Updated: September 11, 2025

### Introduction
ALCHM ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and safety of all users, especially children under 13. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our trauma-informed journaling application.

**IMPORTANT NOTICE: This app is not therapy, medical treatment, or professional mental health care. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact emergency services or a crisis hotline immediately.**

### Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Compliance

#### Age Verification and Parental Consent
- **Users Under 13**: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verified parental consent
- **Age Verification Process**: Users must verify their age during account creation
- **Parental Consent Mechanism**: For users under 13, we require verifiable parental consent before any data collection
- **Consent Methods**: We accept consent via:
  - Signed consent form with government-issued ID verification
  - Video conference with parent/guardian
  - Toll-free number verification with parent/guardian

#### Special Protections for Minors (Under 18)
- **Enhanced Privacy**: Stricter data minimization for users under 18
- **Parental Rights**: Parents/guardians may review, modify, or delete their child's account
- **Limited Data Sharing**: No third-party sharing for users under 18 except for safety purposes
- **Crisis Intervention**: Emergency contacts for users under 18 must include parent/guardian

### Information We Collect

#### Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Email address, display name, age verification
- **Journal Entries**: User-written content for personal reflection (encrypted at rest)
- **Mood Data**: Self-reported emotional states and wellness tracking
- **Usage Analytics**: Anonymized interaction patterns for app improvement

#### AI Processing Data
- **Gemini AI Integration**: Journal summaries are processed by Google's Gemini AI
- **Firebase AI Services**: Automated content analysis for crisis detection
- **Data Processing Location**: AI processing occurs on Google Cloud Platform servers
- **Retention**: AI processing data is deleted within 24 hours of analysis

#### Technical Information
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, app version
- **Performance Data**: App crash reports, loading times, error logs
- **Location Data**: General location (country/state) for crisis resource routing
- **Network Information**: IP address (hashed), connection type

### How We Use Information

#### Primary Purposes
1. **Journaling Services**: Provide secure digital journaling platform
2. **AI Insights**: Generate supportive reflections and growth suggestions
3. **Crisis Detection**: Identify potential safety concerns for intervention
4. **Progress Tracking**: Help users monitor emotional wellness journey
5. **App Improvement**: Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience

#### Crisis Intervention Protocol
- **Automated Detection**: AI systems monitor for crisis language patterns
- **Human Review**: Trained counselors review potential crisis situations
- **Emergency Response**: Contact emergency services when imminent danger detected
- **User Notification**: Users informed of crisis detection and response actions
- **Documentation**: Crisis interventions documented for safety and legal compliance

### Information Sharing and Disclosure

#### We DO NOT Share Personal Information Except:
1. **Crisis Intervention**: When user safety requires emergency response
2. **Legal Compliance**: When required by law enforcement or court order
3. **Service Providers**: Encrypted data with trusted security vendors
4. **Parental Access**: Parents/guardians of users under 18 (limited scope)
5. **User Consent**: When explicit permission granted for specific sharing

#### Third-Party Services
- **Google Firebase**: Hosting, authentication, database services
- **Google Cloud AI**: Gemini AI for journal analysis and insights
- **Stripe**: Payment processing for premium features (encrypted)
- **Crisis Service Integration**: Local crisis hotlines and emergency services

### Data Security and Protection

#### Encryption and Security
- **End-to-End Encryption**: All journal content encrypted with AES-256
- **Secure Transmission**: TLS 1.3 for all data in transit
- **Access Controls**: Multi-factor authentication for staff access
- **Regular Audits**: Quarterly security assessments and penetration testing
- **Data Minimization**: Collect only necessary information for services

#### Crisis Data Handling
- **Privacy-Preserving**: Crisis detection uses AI summaries, not raw content
- **Temporary Storage**: Crisis assessment data deleted within 7 days
- **Professional Standards**: Crisis counselors bound by confidentiality agreements
- **Documentation**: Crisis interventions logged for safety improvement

### User Rights and Controls

#### All Users Can:
- **Access Data**: Request copy of all personal information
- **Correct Information**: Update or modify account information
- **Delete Account**: Permanently remove account and associated data
- **Export Data**: Download journal entries and mood data
- **Opt-Out Analytics**: Disable usage tracking and analytics

#### Additional Rights for EU Users (GDPR)
- **Right to Rectification**: Correct inaccurate personal data
- **Right to Erasure**: "Right to be forgotten" data deletion
- **Right to Portability**: Transfer data to another service
- **Right to Object**: Opt-out of automated decision-making
- **Right to Restrict**: Limit how we process personal data

#### Parental Rights (Users Under 18)
- **Review Child's Data**: Access child's account information (not journal content)
- **Modify Permissions**: Change privacy settings and data sharing preferences
- **Delete Child's Account**: Permanently remove child's account
- **Crisis Contact**: Receive notifications for crisis interventions
- **Consent Management**: Withdraw consent for data processing

### Data Retention and Deletion

#### Retention Periods
- **Journal Entries**: Retained until user deletion or account closure
- **Account Information**: Retained for 30 days after account deletion
- **Analytics Data**: Anonymized data retained for 2 years maximum
- **Crisis Records**: Safety documentation retained for 7 years (legal requirement)
- **Payment Data**: Financial records retained per legal requirements

#### Secure Deletion Process
- **User-Initiated**: Immediate deletion available through account settings
- **Verification**: Email confirmation required for account deletion
- **Backup Removal**: Data removed from all backups within 30 days
- **Third-Party Notification**: Service providers notified to delete shared data
- **Audit Trail**: Deletion activities logged for compliance verification

### International Data Transfers

#### Data Processing Locations
- **Primary Storage**: United States (Firebase servers)
- **AI Processing**: Google Cloud Platform global infrastructure
- **Backup Storage**: Encrypted replicas in multiple jurisdictions
- **Crisis Services**: Data may be shared with local emergency services

#### Transfer Safeguards
- **Standard Contractual Clauses**: EU-approved data transfer mechanisms
- **Adequacy Decisions**: Transfers only to countries with adequate protection
- **Binding Corporate Rules**: Internal policies for international transfers
- **User Consent**: Explicit consent for transfers outside user's country
