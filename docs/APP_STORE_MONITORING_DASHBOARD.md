## ⚠️ Potential Review Issues & Prepared Responses

### Age Rating Concerns
**If Questioned**: "ALCHM is rated 17+ due to mature themes related to trauma recovery and mental health. The app includes comprehensive safety measures, crisis support integration, and is designed specifically for adult users seeking mental health resources."

### AI Functionality Disclosure
**If Questioned**: "ALCHM's AI functionality is clearly disclosed as a supportive wellness tool, not medical advice. Users are repeatedly informed that AI insights supplement, never replace, professional mental health care. Crisis detection immediately connects users to human support."

### Privacy & Data Handling
**If Questioned**: "ALCHM implements military-grade end-to-end encryption with zero-knowledge architecture. Our privacy policy is comprehensive and GDPR/COPPA compliant. We collect minimal data and give users complete control over their information."

### Crisis Support Claims
**If Questioned**: "ALCHM's crisis support features connect users to verified mental health resources and hotlines. We don't provide crisis intervention directly but facilitate immediate access to professional support through established crisis networks."
