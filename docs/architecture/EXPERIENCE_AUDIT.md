# ALCHM Experience Audit

## Experience Question

Would a user feel accompanied, understood, remembered, and welcomed back?

Or would they feel analyzed, managed, optimized, or processed?

## Khepera Experience

Strengths:

- The cognition architecture supports adaptive reasoning instead of templates.
- Safety overrides are explicit.
- Response variety is driven by state, history, and readiness rather than randomness.

Risks:

- Too many reasoning labels could leak into language and make Khepera feel clinical.
- Memory retrieval could feel intrusive if used during activation.
- Intervention rationale should remain internal and never become an explanation of the user.

Experience standard:

Khepera should feel like a witness that remembers the season, not an assistant that analyzes the person.

## Mirror Experience

Strengths:

- Mirror is designed around recognition rather than metrics.
- Movement detection prioritizes emergence, softening, integration, recurrence, and transformation.
- The language model is tentative.

Risks:

- Confidence and recurrence values must stay internal.
- Pattern pages must not become dashboards.
- Synthesis should not over-explain why the user is the way they are.

Experience standard:

Mirror should feel like standing on a quiet hill overlooking one's own life.

## Container Experience

Strengths:

- Containers are framed as lenses and held spaces, not programs.
- Relationship states avoid failure language.
- Re-entry is invitational.

Risks:

- Inquiry progression could accidentally resemble curriculum.
- Container recommendations could become pressure if overused.
- Multiple containers could create choice overload.

Experience standard:

The container waits. It does not count absence.

## Unified Experience

The systems become coherent when:

- the Container changes what Khepera notices
- Khepera creates a safe current reflection
- Mirror notices what changes across time
- Memory provides continuity without exposing raw history

## Implemented Experience Improvement

The cognition adapter makes this chain explicit. It gives future UI and service work a single source for the question: what should be visible now?
