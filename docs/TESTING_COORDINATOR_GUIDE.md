## 🎯 Coordinator Role & Responsibilities Overview

### Primary Responsibilities
The Testing Coordinator serves as the central point of contact for all user testing activities, ensuring participant safety, data quality, and research integrity while maintaining ALCHM's trauma-informed approach.

**Core Functions**:
- **Participant Safety Guardian**: Monitor participant wellbeing throughout testing
- **Data Quality Manager**: Ensure comprehensive, reliable data collection
- **Communication Hub**: Coordinate between participants, research team, and stakeholders
- **Crisis Response Leader**: Execute safety protocols when needed
- **Timeline Manager**: Keep testing on schedule while maintaining quality standards

### Required Qualifications
- **Research Experience**: User research, clinical research, or related field experience
- **Mental Health Literacy**: Understanding of trauma-informed care principles
- **Crisis Response Training**: Mental Health First Aid certification recommended
- **Project Management**: Experience managing complex, multi-stakeholder projects
- **Technology Proficiency**: Comfortable with research tools and data platforms

## 🚨 Crisis Management & Safety Protocols

### Crisis Response Hierarchy

#### Level 1: General Emotional Distress
**Indicators**: Participant reports feeling overwhelmed, sad, or anxious but no immediate safety concerns
**Response Time**: Within 4 hours during business days, within 24 hours on weekends
**Protocol**:
1. Send empathetic response acknowledging their experience
2. Provide general mental health resources and coping strategies
3. Encourage use of existing support systems
4. Monitor for escalation in subsequent communications
5. Document interaction for pattern tracking

#### Level 2: Elevated Concern
**Indicators**: Significant distress, mentions of crisis thoughts, or requesting immediate help
**Response Time**: Within 1 hour, 24/7
**Protocol**:
1. Immediately contact participant via phone/video call
2. Assess immediate safety and crisis risk level
3. Connect to licensed crisis counselor within 2 hours
4. Provide crisis hotline numbers and emergency resources
5. Follow up within 24 hours to ensure safety and support connection

#### Level 3: Crisis Emergency
**Indicators**: Immediate safety concerns, suicide ideation, or imminent self-harm
**Response Time**: Immediately upon identification
**Protocol**:
1. Immediately call emergency services (911) if participant location known
2. Contact participant directly and stay on line until help arrives
3. Engage licensed crisis counselor immediately
4. Notify research team leadership and clinical consultant
5. Document all actions and follow up as directed by crisis professionals

### Safety Monitoring Systems

#### Automated Alert Triggers
- Keywords in survey responses indicating crisis thoughts
- Sudden drop in safety ratings (>2 points day-over-day)
- Explicit requests for help or crisis support
- Participant reports of self-harm or dangerous behaviors
- Technical issues causing participant distress about app security

#### Daily Safety Review Process
1. **Morning Review**: Check all overnight responses for crisis indicators
2. **Keyword Scanning**: Automated system flags concerning language patterns
3. **Rating Analysis**: Identify participants with declining safety or satisfaction scores
4. **Proactive Outreach**: Contact participants showing early warning signs
5. **Clinical Consultation**: Daily check-in with licensed mental health professional

### Crisis Resource Management

#### National Crisis Resources (Always Available)
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**: Call or text 988
- **Crisis Text Line**: Text HOME to 741741
- **National Domestic Violence Hotline**: 1-800-799-7233
- **SAMHSA National Helpline**: 1-800-662-4357

#### Specialized Resources by Population
**LGBTQ+ Participants**: The Trevor Project (1-866-488-7386)
**Veterans**: Veterans Crisis Line (1-800-273-8255, Press 1)
**Spanish-speaking**: Línea Nacional de Prevención del Suicidio (1-800-628-9454)
**College Students**: Campus-specific counseling center resources

#### Professional Support Network
- **Licensed Crisis Counselor**: On-call 24/7 during testing period
- **Clinical Consultant**: Daily availability for safety assessment
- **Research Team Leader**: Always accessible for crisis coordination
- **Legal/Compliance Advisor**: Available for complex safety situations

## 📊 Data Quality Assurance Framework

### Daily Data Quality Checks

#### Participant Response Quality
- **Completion Rates**: Monitor daily survey completion across all participants
- **Response Quality**: Identify extremely brief or repetitive responses needing follow-up
- **Technical Issues**: Track reports of app crashes, data loss, or functionality problems
- **Engagement Authenticity**: Monitor for signs of going-through-motions vs. genuine engagement

#### System Performance Monitoring
- **App Performance**: Track loading times, crash rates, and functionality across devices
- **Survey Platform**: Monitor response submission success and data integrity
- **Video Conferencing**: Test quality and reliability for exit interviews
- **Data Backup**: Verify daily backups and data security protocols

### Weekly Quality Reviews

#### Comprehensive Data Analysis
- **Participation Trends**: Analyze week-over-week engagement patterns
- **Demographic Balance**: Ensure diverse participant representation continues
- **Response Consistency**: Identify participants with inconsistent or concerning response patterns
- **Technical Issue Patterns**: Document systematic problems requiring development attention

#### Research Integrity Assessment
- **Bias Detection**: Monitor for research team bias in participant interactions
- **Protocol Adherence**: Ensure consistent application of procedures across all participants
- **Confidentiality Compliance**: Verify all privacy and data protection standards are met
- **Ethical Standards**: Review all interactions for adherence to trauma-informed principles

## 🔄 Quality Assurance & Continuous Improvement

### Research Quality Standards

#### Trauma-Informed Excellence
- **Safety First**: Every decision prioritizes participant emotional and psychological safety
- **Cultural Humility**: Approaches each participant's experience with respect and openness
- **Power Dynamics**: Maintains awareness of researcher-participant power dynamics
- **Choice and Control**: Ensures participants always feel in control of their participation level

#### Research Rigor Standards
- **Methodology Consistency**: All participants receive identical research protocol
- **Data Integrity**: Multiple verification systems ensure data accuracy and completeness
- **Bias Minimization**: Regular bias checks and multiple-researcher verification
- **Ethical Compliance**: Ongoing adherence to IRB standards and ethical research principles

### Continuous Improvement Process

#### Daily Learning Integration
- **Morning Reflection**: Each day begins with review of previous day's lessons learned
- **Real-Time Adaptation**: Immediate adjustments to address identified issues
- **Participant Feedback Loop**: Rapid incorporation of participant suggestions when appropriate
- **Team Knowledge Sharing**: Daily briefings ensure all team members learn from each interaction

#### Weekly Protocol Refinement
- **Process Review**: Weekly assessment of protocol effectiveness and efficiency
- **Participant Experience Optimization**: Adjustments to improve participant comfort and engagement
- **Data Quality Enhancement**: Continuous improvement of data collection and analysis methods
- **Safety Protocol Updates**: Regular review and enhancement of crisis response procedures

### Research Team Development

#### Skill Building Priorities
- **Trauma-Informed Interviewing**: Advanced training in sensitive topic research methods
- **Crisis Response**: Regular updates on mental health crisis intervention techniques
- **Cultural Competence**: Ongoing education about diverse participant backgrounds and needs
- **Technology Proficiency**: Training on research tools and platforms for maximum efficiency

#### Performance Monitoring
- **Quality Metrics**: Regular assessment of researcher performance against quality standards
- **Participant Feedback**: Monitoring participant comfort and satisfaction with research team interactions
- **Professional Development**: Ongoing training and education opportunities for research team
- **Burnout Prevention**: Regular check-ins on researcher wellbeing and workload management
