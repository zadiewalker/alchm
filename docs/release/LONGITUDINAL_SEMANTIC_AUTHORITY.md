## Fail-Closed Requirements

- A disabled or absent semantic transition must not be approximated by a
  client write.
- A source implementation does not become user-visible without runtime
  evidence, a verifier-issued receipt path, and an approved release decision.
- No transition may interpret emotional development or reconstruct meaning
  from stored writing without separately reviewed authority.
- Export/deletion coverage must be defined before new continuity state is made
  available.
