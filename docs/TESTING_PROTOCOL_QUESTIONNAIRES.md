# 📋 ALCHM Testing Protocol & Questionnaires

## 🛡️ **SAFETY-FIRST TESTING PROTOCOL**

### **Phase 1: Pre-Testing Safety Setup**

#### **Safety Screening Call (15 minutes)**
**Conducted by:** Licensed crisis counselor or trained mental health professional

**Script Opening:**
"Thank you for your interest in ALCHM beta testing. This call ensures your safety and helps us understand how to best support you during testing. Everything we discuss is confidential, and there's no pressure to continue if you're not comfortable."

**Key Assessment Areas:**
1. **Current Stability Check**
2. **Crisis Support System Validation** 
3. **Technology Comfort Assessment**
4. **Informed Consent Process**
5. **Emergency Protocol Setup**

### **Phase 2: Technical Onboarding (30 minutes)**

#### **Setup Checklist:**
- [ ] App installation and account creation
- [ ] Privacy settings walkthrough
- [ ] Crisis resource test (non-emergency simulation)
- [ ] Emergency contact verification
- [ ] Weekly check-in scheduling

### **Phase 3: Active Testing (4 weeks)**

#### **Daily Protocol:**
- Journaling use at participant's own pace
- Automatic crisis monitoring (24/7)
- Optional daily mood check-in (30 seconds)

#### **Weekly Protocol:**
- Safety check-in survey (5 minutes)
- Optional feedback call (15 minutes)
- Crisis counselor review of flagged content

## 📊 **DATA ANALYSIS PLAN**

### **Quantitative Analysis**
- Safety metrics: Crisis detection accuracy, response times
- Usability metrics: Task completion rates, navigation success
- Performance metrics: Loading times, error rates
- Engagement metrics: Usage frequency, session duration

### **Qualitative Analysis**
- Thematic analysis of open-ended responses
- Cultural sensitivity assessment across demographics
- Trauma-informed design effectiveness evaluation
- Safety protocol improvement identification

### **Mixed-Methods Integration**
- Triangulation of quantitative and qualitative findings
- Demographic breakdown of all metrics
- Cultural community-specific analysis
- Professional validation of clinical insights

This comprehensive testing protocol ensures rigorous evaluation while maintaining participant safety and cultural sensitivity.
