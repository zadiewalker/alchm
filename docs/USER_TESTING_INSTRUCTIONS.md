## 📱 **TESTING PROTOCOL:**

### **Phase 1: Close Friends (5 people, 48 hours)**
- Send to your most trusted friends
- Ask for brutally honest feedback
- Focus on finding major bugs/issues

### **Phase 2: Extended Network (10-15 people, 1 week)**  
- Share with colleagues, acquaintances
- Test different demographics
- Refine based on Phase 1 feedback

### **Phase 3: Mental Health Community (25+ people, 2 weeks)**
- Share in appropriate mental health communities
- Get feedback from your target audience
- Validate trauma-informed approach

## 🔍 **KEY QUESTIONS TO ASK TESTERS:**

1. **First Impression**: What was your immediate reaction?
2. **Ease of Use**: How intuitive was the interface?
3. **AI Response**: Did the AI feel helpful and trauma-informed?
4. **Trust & Safety**: Would you feel safe sharing personal thoughts?
5. **Mobile Experience**: How was it on your phone?
6. **Biggest Issue**: What frustrated you most?
7. **Best Feature**: What did you love?
