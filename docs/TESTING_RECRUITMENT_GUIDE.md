# 🌿 ALCHM Beta Testing Recruitment Guide

## 📢 **PRIMARY RECRUITMENT MESSAGE**

**Be Part of Something Life-Changing**

ALCHM is seeking compassionate beta testers to help us perfect the world's first trauma-informed digital journaling sanctuary.

**What is ALCHM?**
A safe, private space for healing through writing - designed specifically for people who have experienced trauma, stress, or difficult life transitions.

**Why Your Voice Matters:**
Your lived experience will help us create a digital sanctuary that truly understands and supports vulnerable users during their most difficult moments.

## 🛡️ **SAFETY-FOCUSED MESSAGING**

### **Crisis Intervention Transparency**

**"Your Safety is Our Priority"**

ALCHM's beta testing includes comprehensive safety protocols:
- ✅ **24/7 Crisis Support:** Licensed crisis counselors monitoring
- ✅ **Immediate Intervention:** Direct connection to 988 and local resources
- ✅ **Professional Oversight:** Mental health professionals reviewing features
- ✅ **Informed Consent:** Full transparency about crisis detection
- ✅ **Right to Withdraw:** Exit anytime with continued support

**Privacy Protection:**
"Your journal entries are encrypted and private. Crisis detection only processes AI-generated summaries, ensuring personal thoughts remain confidential while keeping you safe."

## 📋 **SCREENING PROCESS**

### **Safety Assessment Questions:**
1. Current mental health stability (1-5 scale)
2. Current therapy/treatment status
3. Recent self-harm thoughts (past 30 days)
4. Existing safety plan/support system
5. Specific trauma triggers to be aware of

### **Demographics (Optional):**
- Age range, gender identity, sexual orientation
- Racial/ethnic identity, primary language
- Immigration status, religious/spiritual identity
- Technology comfort and device access

### **Consent Requirements:**
- [ ] Understanding of AI crisis detection
- [ ] Consent to immediate crisis intervention
- [ ] Anonymous data collection agreement
- [ ] Right to withdraw acknowledgment

## 🎯 **SUCCESS METRICS**

### **Target Numbers:**
- **Total Participants:** 40-50 beta testers
- **Vulnerable Youth (16-25):** 15-20 participants
- **Trauma Survivors:** 10-15 participants
- **Cultural Communities:** 8-12 participants
- **Professional/Technical:** 5-8 participants

### **Diversity Goals:**
- **Gender:** 30% LGBTQ+ including transgender representation
- **Race/Ethnicity:** 40% BIPOC across communities
- **Age:** 60% ages 16-35, 40% ages 35+
- **Geography:** 30% rural/small town, 70% urban/suburban
- **Language:** 20% non-native English speakers

### **Timeline:**
- **Week 1:** Professional outreach and partnerships
- **Week 2:** Community recruitment and social media
- **Week 3:** Individual outreach and screening
- **Week 4:** Final recruitment and testing prep

This recruitment guide ensures diverse, safe, and representative participation in ALCHM's beta testing program.
