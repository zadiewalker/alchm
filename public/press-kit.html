Company Overview

ALCHM is revolutionizing mental health technology with trauma-informed AI that actually understands healing

The Problem We Solve

Traditional mental health apps ignore the complexities of trauma. They push toxic positivity, oversimplify healing, and often retraumatize users with insensitive AI responses. 94% of trauma survivors report feeling misunderstood by existing digital wellness tools.

Our Revolutionary Solution

ALCHM is the first AI journaling platform built from the ground up with trauma-informed principles. Our proprietary AI understands that healing isn't linear, validates complex emotions, and provides the safety trauma survivors need to process difficult experiences.

What Makes Us Different

  • Trauma-Informed AI: Trained on therapeutic principles, never dismissive or harmful
  • Grace-Based Gamification: Celebrates progress without shame or pressure
  • Crisis Prevention: AI monitors for distress and provides immediate resources
  • Privacy-First: Enterprise-grade security for vulnerable thoughts
