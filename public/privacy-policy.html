ALCHM Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 19, 2025

Overview

ALCHM is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our trauma-informed journaling application.

Data We Collect

ALCHM collects minimal data necessary to provide our journaling and AI-assisted reflection services:

  • Journal Entries: Your written reflections and journaling content
  • Mood Trends: Emotional state indicators and wellness tracking data
  • Usage Analytics: Basic app usage patterns to improve our services
  • Account Information: Email address and authentication data for account management

How We Process Your Data

Your data is processed via Firebase and Genkit technologies:

  • Firebase: Secure cloud storage and authentication services
  • Genkit: AI-powered reflection assistance and content analysis
  • Local Processing: Where possible, data processing occurs on your device

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption at Rest: All stored data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Encryption in Transit: Data transmission protected by TLS/HTTPS protocols
  • Access Controls: Strict authentication and authorization mechanisms
  • Regular Audits: Continuous security monitoring and compliance checks

Data Sharing

ALCHM does not share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • When legally required by court order or government regulation
  • In emergency situations to prevent harm to you or others
  • With your explicit consent for specific features or integrations

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request copies of your personal data
  • Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your account and data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Portability: Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdrawal: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary:

  • Journal entries: Until you delete them or close your account
  • Analytics data: Maximum 26 months for trend analysis
  • Account data: Until account deletion is requested

Children's Privacy

ALCHM is designed for users 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in servers located outside your country of residence. We ensure adequate protection through:

  • Compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations
  • Standard contractual clauses with service providers
  • Regular privacy impact assessments

Third-Party Services

ALCHM utilizes the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): For backend services and authentication
  • Genkit (Google): For AI-powered content analysis

Please refer to Firebase's Privacy Policy for additional details on how Google processes data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Notify you via email or in-app notification
  • Update the effective date at the top of this policy
  • Provide a summary of key changes
