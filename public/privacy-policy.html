Overview

ALCHM Digital Sanctuary is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data. As a trauma-informed journaling platform serving vulnerable populations, particularly youth aged 17-25, we implement the highest standards of privacy protection while providing life-critical mental health support.

Privacy Excellence Commitment

✅ GDPR Compliant: Full compliance with European data protection standards
✅ COPPA Certified: Enhanced protections for users under 18
✅ HIPAA Aligned: Healthcare-grade security for therapist integration
✅ Privacy-by-Design: Privacy protection built into every feature
✅ Crisis Safety: Life-critical systems with privacy preservation

Data We Collect

ALCHM follows strict data minimization principles, collecting only essential data to provide our trauma-informed journaling and crisis safety services:

Essential Platform Data

  • Account Information: Email address and secure authentication data for account management
  • Age Verification: Age group classification (not exact birthdate) for regulatory compliance
  • Journal Entries: Your encrypted journaling content (protected by client-side encryption)
  • Wellness Patterns: Anonymized emotional trends and wellness indicators

Crisis Safety Data (Life-Critical Protection)

  • Crisis Indicators: Anonymized patterns for safety monitoring (no raw content processed)
  • Emergency Contacts: Contacts you designate for crisis intervention (encrypted)
  • Safety Preferences: Your crisis response preferences and consent settings
  • Intervention Records: Documentation of crisis support provided (retained per legal requirements)

Optional Enhanced Features (Explicit Consent Required)

  • AI Insights: Anonymized emotional patterns for personalized insights (opt-in only)
  • Therapist Integration: HIPAA-compliant sharing with licensed mental health providers (opt-in only)
  • Research Participation: Fully anonymized data for mental health research (opt-in only)
  • Platform Analytics: Usage patterns to improve platform safety and functionality

How We Process Your Data

ALCHM processes your data using privacy-preserving technologies and industry-leading security measures:

Privacy-by-Design Architecture

  • Client-Side Encryption: Journal content encrypted on your device before transmission
  • Zero-Knowledge Server: Our servers cannot access your encrypted journal content
  • Privacy-Preserving Crisis Detection: Safety monitoring without accessing raw content
  • Data Minimization: Only essential data processed for each specific purpose
  • Automatic Deletion: Data automatically deleted based on retention policies

Secure Infrastructure

  • Firebase (Google Cloud): SOC 2 certified cloud infrastructure with comprehensive Data Processing Agreement
  • End-to-End Encryption: AES-256 encryption for all data at rest and in transit
  • Multi-Factor Authentication: Enhanced security for all user accounts
  • Regular Security Audits: External security assessments and penetration testing

AI Processing with Maximum Privacy Protection

  • No Raw Content Access: AI systems never receive your actual journal entries
  • Differential Privacy: Mathematical privacy guarantees for pattern analysis
  • Local Processing: Analysis happens on your device when possible
  • Anonymized Indicators Only: Only anonymous emotional patterns shared for insights
  • User Control: Complete control over AI feature activation and data sharing
  • Opt-Out Available: Disable AI processing while maintaining full platform functionality

Crisis Safety Processing (Life-Critical Systems)

  • Privacy-Preserving Detection: Pattern recognition without content analysis
  • Human Verification: Crisis counselor confirmation before intervention
  • Emergency Override: Limited privacy override only for imminent life danger
  • Comprehensive Documentation: All crisis interventions fully documented
  • Cultural Competency: Culturally responsive crisis intervention protocols

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption at Rest: All stored data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Encryption in Transit: Data transmission protected by TLS/HTTPS protocols
  • Access Controls: Strict authentication and authorization mechanisms
  • Regular Audits: Continuous security monitoring and compliance checks

Data Sharing

ALCHM does not share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • When legally required by court order or government regulation
  • In emergency situations to prevent harm to you or others
  • With your explicit consent for specific features or integrations

Data Retention and Deletion

ALCHM follows strict data minimization principles with automatic deletion schedules:

User-Controlled Retention

  • Journal Entries: Retained until you delete them or close your account
  • Account Data: Retained during account lifetime, deleted within 30 days of account closure
  • Privacy Settings: You control retention periods for different data categories
  • Immediate Deletion: One-click deletion available for all personal data

Automatic Deletion Schedules

  • Crisis Indicators: Automatically deleted after 30 days (14 days for minors)
  • Session Data: Deleted after 7 days of inactivity
  • Analytics Data: Anonymized after 6 months, deleted after 26 months
  • Support Tickets: Deleted after 2 years unless ongoing legal matter

Legal Retention Requirements

  • Crisis Intervention Records: 7 years (healthcare documentation requirements)
  • HIPAA Records: 6 years (healthcare compliance)
  • Payment Records: 7 years (tax and accounting requirements)
  • Audit Logs: 7 years (regulatory compliance)

Enhanced Protections for Minors

  • Reduced Retention: Shorter retention periods for users under 18
  • Priority Deletion: Expedited deletion processing for minor accounts
  • Parental Rights: Parents can request deletion for children under 16
  • Age-Out Protection: Special deletion rights when turning 18

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Age Verification: ALCHM is designed for users 17 years and older. We implement robust age verification to ensure compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and App Store guidelines.

COPPA Protection Measures:

  • Age Verification: All users must verify they are 17 or older before account creation
  • No Collection from Minors: We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 17
  • Parental Rights: If we discover we have collected information from someone under 17, we will delete it immediately
  • Safe Discovery Process: If you believe we have collected information from a minor, contact us at privacy@alchm.com
  • Educational Use: For users 17-18, additional safeguards apply including educational resource prioritization

Youth Safety Protections (Ages 17-25):

  • Enhanced Crisis Detection: Additional monitoring for youth-specific crisis indicators
  • Educational Resource Priority: Crisis resources include youth-specific support services
  • Trauma-Informed Design: Special consideration for developmental trauma and identity formation
  • Parental Involvement Options: For users 17-18, optional family involvement in crisis situations (with user consent)

Medical Disclaimer

IMPORTANT: NOT MEDICAL ADVICE

ALCHM is a journaling and self-reflection tool, NOT a medical or therapeutic service.

  • Emergency Situations: If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or 911 immediately
  • Professional Help: ALCHM does not replace professional mental health treatment, therapy, or medical care
  • AI Limitations: Our AI systems provide supportive insights only and are not qualified to diagnose or treat mental health conditions
  • Crisis Detection: While we monitor for crisis indicators, this is not a guarantee of intervention or professional care
  • User Responsibility: Users are responsible for seeking appropriate professional help when needed

Crisis Intervention Limitations

While ALCHM includes crisis detection and resource provision:

  • No Guarantee: Crisis detection is not foolproof and should not be relied upon as sole safety measure
  • User Initiative: Users must take initiative to seek help when experiencing crisis
  • Professional Networks: Crisis resources connect to professional services, but ALCHM staff are not mental health professionals
  • Liability: ALCHM is not liable for outcomes related to mental health episodes or crisis situations
