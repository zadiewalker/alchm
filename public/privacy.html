6. Crisis Intervention

When our AI systems detect potential crisis indicators:

  • Crisis assessments are logged for follow-up care

7. Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Data retained for service provision
  • Inactive Accounts: Deleted after 2 years of inactivity
  • Crisis Logs: Retained for 7 years for safety compliance
  • Analytics: Anonymized data may be retained indefinitely

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

⚠️ Age Requirement: ALCHM is designed for users 17+ years old due to mature content themes related to trauma recovery and mental health.

For users under 17:

  • ALCHM is not intended for use by minors under 17
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 17
  • If we become aware that a child under 17 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately
  • Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided personal information should contact us immediately

For users 17-18 years old:

  • Enhanced privacy protections automatically apply
  • Crisis intervention protocols include emergency contact notification
  • Additional consent verification may be required
  • Parents/guardians may request access to account information
