## 📱 TestFlight Testing Strategy

### Testing Phases
**Phase 1: Internal Testing (You + Core Team)**
- Duration: 2-3 days
- Focus: Core functionality, new features
- Test all Build #3 improvements

**Phase 2: Friends & Family Beta**
- Duration: 1 week

**Phase 3: External Beta**
